Privacy.

Plasma Remote, for iPhone and Android, controls Plasma on your TV. It collects nothing about you, and it talks to nothing but your TV.

What stays on your phone

The TVs you pair with: each one’s name, its address on your network, the fingerprint of its certificate, and the key it gave your phone. They’re kept in the iPhone’s Keychain, or sealed with a key from Android’s Keystore, and they stay on this phone: they aren’t backed up to the cloud or moved to a new phone. Your settings, such as Haptics, are kept with the app.

What goes to your TV

The name you give your phone when you pair it, which the TV shows in its list of phones, and what you do in the app: the keys you press, the channels and titles you pick, and the text you type. It goes over your home network, encrypted, to your TV alone.

Nothing else

No account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reports. The app doesn’t contact any server: the channels’ logos and the posters come from your TV. Source Code, the licenses and this page, in the app’s Settings, open in your browser.

Your camera

Only to scan the code your TV shows, when you choose Scan Code. The pictures are read on your phone, and they aren’t kept or sent anywhere.

Your local network

To find the TVs running Plasma on your Wi-Fi, and to connect to the one you pick. On iPhone, the app asks you first.

Forgetting a TV

Settings › Paired TVs › Forget deletes a TV and its key from your phone, and deleting the app deletes everything it kept. On the TV, Settings › Remote & Buttons › Phone Remote forgets a phone.

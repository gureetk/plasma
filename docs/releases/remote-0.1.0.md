The first release of Plasma Remote, the phone remote for Plasma, for Android and iPhone.

Plasma Remote does what Plasma's web phone remote does, and more: it finds the TVs running Plasma on your Wi-Fi by itself, pairs by scanning the code your TV shows, keeps a connection encrypted and pinned to your TV's own certificate, opens the keyboard by itself when the TV wants text, and gives a light tap with each key.

## What's in it

- **Remote:** the D-pad and OK, or a swipe pad; Back, Home, Menu and number entry; rewind, play and pause, fast-forward, channel up and down; the four color keys, named for the screen you're on; what's playing, with Go Live.
- **Channels:** your lists and channels as the TV numbers them, with their logos and what's on. A tap tunes.
- **Movies and Series:** your categories with posters, as the TV shows them to the profile watching. A tap opens a title's page on the TV; a long press plays it.
- **Type:** the TV's text field on your phone, with the keyboard up.
- **Settings:** Haptics, Keep Screen On, the swipe pad, your paired TVs with Forget, Try the Demo, and About.

It needs the Plasma release after 1.0.0 on the TV (1.0.0 has the web page's remote, not the apps' encrypted connection, nor Movies and Series), with Settings › Remote & Buttons › Phone Remote on.

## Installing

- **Android:** `plasma-remote-0.1.0.apk` below, on Android 8.0 or later. F-Droid comes later.
- **iPhone:** TestFlight, by invitation, on iOS 16 or later.

Plasma Remote collects nothing about you and talks only to your TV: [its privacy policy](https://plasma.gureet.ca/remote-privacy.html).
